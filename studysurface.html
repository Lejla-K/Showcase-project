Corrected by the design system file

  • Inter, not the grotesque I guessed at. The library has font-family tokens for Body, Display, Display (Alt) and Display (Brand), and a scale of Display 1–3 / Title 1–2 / Body 1–3 / Caption 1–2.
  • Everything uses their Progress component. Eight steps at 12.5% each, plus Completed and Repeat. That now covers the day, the prep stages, and count-based goals — the donut is gone, since it was a shape the library doesn't have. One consequence to confirm: a 12-task day can't land on an eighth, so the meter rounds to the nearest step while the fraction stays exact. The Repeat state still needs designing into this screen.
  • Colour belongs to prep stages. Each stage has main and accent ramps in Default / LC / HC / Overlay, documented as identity vs contrast. So CLIR is carried by letters on neutral chips, not by colour — colour is already spoken for.
  • Open question: the library has two generations, Simple and Expressive, plus a dark mode. This is drawn Expressive, matching the current My Plan page. Confirm before building.

Fixed from the heuristic pass

  • One day control, not two. A pill reading "Wednesday, Jul 15" and a button reading "Today" said the same thing. The date pill is now the only statement of which day you are on, flanked by ‹ ›; "Back to today" appears only once you have navigated away. Page to another day to see it.
  • The CLIR dot explains itself on hover rather than needing a legend elsewhere on the page. The trade-off is that the meaning is hover-only, so it doesn't reach touch — same unresolved issue as the plan strip. Both need one answer, not two.
  • The ⋯ is always visible. Hover-only meant touch users had no route to reschedule or skip.
  • Checkbox and launch arrow are separated, and the checkbox is larger. Mis-tapping "start" when you meant "done" navigates you away.
  • Still open: the strip and mile markers remain hover-dependent, so on touch they carry no information. Needs a non-hover route — tap-to-expand, or the dates written out.

Proposals, not readings of the PRD

  • The goal ladder sits in the rail, with the Prep Map. The PRD puts it atop the day timeline, but a goal advances on performance over weeks — it is not a property of Wednesday. Left column is longitudinal, main column is today.
  • LR and RC share one control. Both goals are always live; the switch is a viewing control, not a state. When both sit on the same rung, the switch shouldn't render at all.
  • Goal readouts vary by measure. A time goal is lower-is-better, so a percentage bar is meaningless without a stored baseline — it shows 31:40 → 25:00. Counts get boxes. Percentages get a bar.
  • A day close, not a task. The Tandem Plan ends every day with a reflection, which the PRD doesn't mention. It appears once the day is checked off, as pills you can pick several of, or skip.
  • Not designed: the celebration moment (Ellen's), real copy, the ⋯ popover contents, error and locked states, the upsell behind "Include book". Redline Prep is a placeholder business name.
